Use a structured 60-90 second intro with a clear ending

Your answer lasted about 79s but was fragmented: education, then India, then projects, then publication, then it trailed off ('Yeah, that's it'). Use a present-past-future structure: who you are now (MS in Cybersecurity), what you've done (SOC work, Splunk lab, phishing detection publication), and why this SOC Analyst role is the next step. 5-minute drill: Record yourself giving the intro in three parts, each 20-30 seconds, using bullet notes. Replay and check it ends on a sentence about the role, not a fade-out.

Fix pace, opening delay and eye contact

Your first word came after 8.7s, your pace was 99 wpm, and you had a pause of 8.6s, which makes you sound unsure even when the content is good.
